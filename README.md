# screenwritertool_AI
A modern screenplay editor with AI-assisted writing, scene management, idea organization, and screenplay formatting. This is the tool for the people who are begineer in screenwriting this tool will help them create a structured and great screenplay using the tools and techniques with assistance from ai for the effective writing.

Screenplay writing can be a complicated process, even if you have an excellent idea. You may not know how to develop it, format it properly, or choose the right words.

For this reason, This ScreenWriterTool  was created.

ScreenWriterTool is a screenplay writing tool designed for beginners who want to learn and improve their screenwriting skills while working on their own scripts.

This tool is not meant to create the entire script for you. Instead, it acts as a creative companion, helping you structure your screenplay, organize scenes, develop ideas, and improve descriptions.

You remain in complete control of the plot, characters, dialogue, and every creative decision in your story. The goal is to help writers express their ideas more clearly while preserving their own voice and style.
